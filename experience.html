Portfolio Assistant Manager
Lu Family Real Estate Trust
2023 – Present

Managing a 16-property real estate portfolio

I started helping with the family real estate portfolio at fourteen, mostly reading leases. It became a real job: I now manage 16 properties across Chandler, Gilbert, Maricopa, and Casa Grande, covering 60+ tenants. I renegotiated vendor contracts that had gone unreviewed for years, which alone freed up meaningful margin, and I built a set of Excel dashboards from scratch to track rent collection, maintenance costs, and lease renewal timing in one place instead of across scattered spreadsheets and paper files.

The part that changed how I think about the work wasn’t the spreadsheets, though — it was the tenants. I’ve helped a single mother in Casa Grande access the DES Emergency Rental Assistance Program when she fell behind, and coordinated Section 8 and Tenant-Based Rental Assistance paperwork for disabled tenants in Chandler. Running a portfolio well isn’t just about the NOI line. It's making sure the systems in place actually catch the people who need them.

Research Intern
Center for Photonics Innovation, Arizona State University
2024 – Present

Simulating semiconductors before anyone builds them

Working under Dr. Shane Johnson, I study the Molecular Beam Epitaxy (MBE) growth of III-V compound semiconductor materials and use COMSOL Multiphysics to model optoelectronic devices — strain-balanced type-II InAs/InAsSb superlattice photosensors, specifically. The appeal of this kind of work is that you can test a design decision computationally before anyone spends money fabricating it. That habit — simulate first, build second — is exactly what carried over into the patent Jaden and I later filed.

This role produced three of my seven research publications, presented at SPIE, IEEE ITherm, and AVS — three of the more established venues in optoelectronics and thermal engineering.
