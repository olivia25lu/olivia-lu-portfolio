Founder & President
Hope in the Desert Foundation
2023 – Present

Founder of a 501(c)(3) nonprofit

I founded Hope in the Desert to bring academic support and essential resources to underprivileged K–12 students across Arizona, and built it into an organization directing 100+ members and 20+ ambassadors. Its flagship program is a citywide after-school tutoring initiative at Basha Public Library, serving 200+ underserved and immigrant students through roughly 70 trained tutors delivering 4,000+ volunteer hours — with materials I designed specifically for ESL and ADHD-sensitive instruction, now used library-wide.

The foundation also runs two partner initiatives: Arizona Students Recycling Used Technology, refurbishing 100+ devices for Title I schools and low-income families (which is how AlgebraXpert, the math app I built, ended up in students’ hands), and a collaboration with Ronald McDonald House Charities, where I directed 80+ volunteers assembling and delivering 100+ wellness kits funded by $6,000 in donations.

City of Chandler Mayor’s Youth Commission
2023 – Present

Three years climbing a 19-seat civic advisory board

I joined as a Commissioner, one of 19 youth representatives advising the Mayor and City Council on civic initiatives. By 11th grade I was Secretary, coordinating 37 civic engagement projects and events. As Chair in 12th grade, I led meetings, directed projects, and introduced a pilot program giving youth commissioners actual seats on city boards — Parks & Recreation, Housing, Human Services — rather than a purely advisory role.

Two moments stand out: overseeing the Teen Leadership Academy, which walked 65+ students through how local government actually works, and being selected as the sole youth delegate to Chandler’s Bloomberg Harvard Resident Ideation Session, where I helped shape a 3-1-1 civic service system proposal for a city of 250,000+ residents.

Founder & President
National Economics & Personal Finance Challenge Club
2023 – Present

10 members to 100+ in three months — and 20% to 70% female participation

This club existed before I took it over, but barely. I redesigned recruitment and rebuilt the curriculum around weekly, accessible financial-literacy lessons — budgeting, credit, loan management — specifically aimed at students who’d never taken a formal economics class, including underrepresented and immigrant students. Membership grew tenfold in three months, and female participation went from a fifth of the club to more than two-thirds, with 10 of 15 teams now majority-girl and majority-minority.

I mentored 12 competition teams to state and national placement. One all-girls team I coached won the state round and advanced to Nationals; a team of minority students won the Arizona Personal Finance Challenge outright and placed Top 20 nationally. The club was eventually featured on our school’s newscast for its work making economics education genuinely inclusive.

President
Junior Investors League
2023 – Present

Leading a competitive investment club

Junior Investors League is built around Wharton’s Global High School Investment Challenge, and under my leadership it doubled member retention year-over-year while running weekly portfolio-management and risk-analysis workshops for 40+ students. I personally directed our sector research and equity coverage of technology and consumer stocks — work that helped the team rank in the Top 150 of over 1,600 teams worldwide — while managing logistics and communications across 30+ working sessions.
